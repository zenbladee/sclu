DETENTION FREE SAN DIEGO

Students' Civil Liberties Union is working on placing signage, stickers and placards outside public locations such as local businesses, offices, shops, and schools. These signs would indicate specific areas inside are marked as private and act as sanctuary spaces for immigrants. These are areas immigration enforcement cannot enter without a warrant. This campaign also works on safeguarding the rights and successes of undocumented students and community members in San Diego. Thus, for the past few months, SCLU has been canvassing businesses in the City of San Diego to put up signage. If you are interested in supporting/promoting this campaign, or would like to procure signage for your public location, please email us with "Detention Free SD" in the subject line at sclu.sd@proton.me - or schedule a meeting with the button below.

SAY NO TO SURVEILLANCE

Alongside the San Diego TRUST Coalition, Students' Civil Liberties Union has been working on supporting the removal of Flock-connected Automated License Plate Reader Cameras across San Diego (and in California). Flock controls a nation-wide database of similar cameras that is infamous for data breaches, wrongful access and illegal data-sharing practices that harm our student, immigrant and overpoliced communities. If you are interested in supporting/promoting this campaign please email us with "Say No to Surveillance" in the subject line at sclu.sd@proton.me - or schedule a meeting with the button below.

BLIND SNAKES DISABILITY JUSTICE CAMPAIGN

After years of continuous advocacy, our organizers as well as the newly-integrated Blind Snakes Co-operative successfully helped UC San Diego establish the Disability Resource Hub. Our disability justice campaign currently focuses on building mutual aid and community centers for disabled youth across San Diego, as well as informing the academic realm with the creation of a disability studies minor in San Diegan colleges.
