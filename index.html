Empowering Students: Youth Advocacy in San Diego

Students' Civil Liberties Union (SCLU) is a dedicated youth-led nonprofit organization based in San Diego, CA.

We are an incubator for youth organizing projects, campaigns and movements that aim to combat youth apathy, building youth power through multigenerational coalitions for democratic renewal.

What We Do

Policy Advocacy and Reform

Working with decisionmakers to influence legislation and administrative policy to protect and enhance civil liberties for students. Researching, deliberating and creating systems to ensure concerns are addressed.

Explore our Campaigns

Community Outreach

Engaging with local communities to raise awareness and foster support for immigrant and disabled students’ rights. We also ensure that the institutions of a community develop to provide the right care and concern for these issues.

Explore our Resources

Develop Youth Leaders as Organizers

We help facilitate and co-create campaigns with youth leaders to develop organizing initiatives, community-led programs and changemaking projects across San Diego.

Our Current Campaigns

Detention Free San Diego

Students' Civil Liberties Union is working on placing signage, stickers and placards outside public locations such as local businesses, offices, shops, and schools.

Learn More

Say NO to Surveillance

Alongside the San Diego TRUST Coalition, Students' Civil Liberties Union has been working on supporting the removal of Flock-connected Automated License Plate Reader Cameras across San Diego (and in California).

Learn More

Blind Snakes Disability Justice

After years of continuous advocacy, our organizers as well as the newly-integrated Blind Snakes Co-operative successfully helped UC San Diego establish the Disability Resource Hub.
